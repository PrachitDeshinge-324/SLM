Future Research Pathways

Executive Summary (updated after the audit)

The earlier plan (self-improvement loop on Qwen 0.8B, or a harder dataset for DeepSeek) is superseded. After fixing the scoring, 8-bit is safe, 4-bit costs accuracy mainly in weak models, and the model-as-judge is weak everywhere. The promising result is that the drop in self-agreement tracks the drop in accuracy (r = 0.93 over 20 runs). Next: confirm it with a second quantizer, a 3-bit point and three seeds. See Audit & New Direction.

Step 1: Clean rerun
  • Use the fixed prompts (no double start token, pinned Llama date, DeepSeek instructions in the user turn, shared SVAMP prompt).
  • Raise the token limit until truncation is below 5%.
  • Run all precisions of a model together; three seeds; 100–200 questions.
Step 2: More damage to measure
  • Add GPTQ or GGUF Q4 and a 3-bit point.
  • Keep the models where 4-bit actually hurts (Qwen-0.8B, Gemma-1B) and add one more weak family.
Step 3: Test the estimator
  • Predict each quantized model's accuracy change from its agreement change, on held-out models.
  • Beat two label-free baselines: flip rate against 16-bit, and cross-model disagreement.
  • Falsified if the held-out error is no better than "assume no change".
Later: Experiment B

The self-improvement training loop is still unrun. Only start it if the estimator holds up, and track vote accuracy against sample accuracy each round to catch collapse early.

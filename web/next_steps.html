Future Research Pathways

Executive Summary

Based on Experiment D, Qwen 0.8B is the only viable candidate for autonomous self-improvement under quantization. The next immediate step is to either (A) execute the full QLoRA self-training loop to see if the 4-bit FPR spike poisons the model, or (B) swap to a harder dataset to measure DeepSeek without the ceiling effect.

Option A: Experiment B (Self-Improvement Loop)

The original proposal outlined moving from Inference-only verification (Experiment D) to an actual Self-Improvement Training Loop (Experiment B).

  • Take the quantized Qwen 0.8B model.
  • Generate multiple samples, keep the ones matching the majority-vote answer.
  • Fine-tune (using QLoRA) on those self-generated, self-approved traces.
  • Compare the resulting model's accuracy after 1-3 iterations against the quantized baseline. Does the training loop break down, or does the 8-bit regularization hold up?
Option B: New Dataset Sweet Spot

Because GSM8K generated a ceiling effect for models like DeepSeek-1.5B (90% accuracy), we are unable to accurately measure its verifier usefulness.

  • Run Experiment D again on a slightly harder dataset like EleutherAI/asdiv (Asian Math Word Problems) or MAWPS.
  • Find the dataset where DeepSeek's base accuracy sits around 60-70%.
  • This will remove the "Shrinking Denominator" effect and allow us to cleanly measure its False Positive Rate without class imbalance distortion.

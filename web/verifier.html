Phase 2: Comprehensive Verifier Findings

Executive Summary: 4 Major Discoveries

Can quantized SLMs self-improve? Our evaluation uncovered four critical insights for edge AI:

  • 1. The 4-bit Lobotomy (Quantization Effect): 4-bit quantization destroys a model's ability to verify logic. Look at Gemma-1B or DeepSeek-1.5B: their MCC drops sharply in 4-bit, proving quantization breaks internal logical coherence long before it breaks generation capacity.
  • 2. The MCC / Imbalance Problem: When a generator is highly accurate (e.g., DeepSeek at 90%), FPR artificially spikes because there are almost no negative examples. Using Matthew's Correlation Coefficient (MCC) reveals that DeepSeek is actually a phenomenal judge, immune to the "Yes Man" effect.
  • 3. The "Yes Man" Effect: Sub-2B standard models blindly rubber-stamp their own hallucinations. Feeding this data into a self-improvement loop (STaR) will cause catastrophic model collapse.
  • 4. The ~3B Parameter Survival Line: Scale acts as a buffer. Llama-3.2-3B and Qwen3.5-2B are the only non-reasoning models capable of surviving quantization while maintaining a positive verification Delta.

1. Key Self-Improvement Metrics

1. Verifier Accuracy Delta (Δ)
$$\Delta = \text{Accuracy}_{\text{Verifier}} - \text{Accuracy}_{\text{Generator}}$$

The Real-World Test: A negative Δ means the verifier actively hurts performance and cannot be used for self-improvement.

2. Matthew's Correlation Coefficient (MCC)
$$MCC = \frac{TP \times TN - FP \times FN}{\sqrt{(TP+FP)(TP+FN)(TN+FP)(TN+FN)}}$$

The Ground Truth: FPR is highly sensitive to class imbalance (if Gen Acc is 90%, True Negatives are scarce, artificially spiking FPR). MCC considers all four quadrants, making it the mathematically superior metric for judging a verifier's true competence.

3. Deep Dive Insights

Insight 1: The 4-bit Lobotomy

Look at the MCC column for Gemma-3-1B or DeepSeek. As precision drops from 16-bit to 4-bit, the MCC collapses.

Conclusion: 4-bit quantization destroys the continuous weight representations required to maintain a logical chain of thought. You cannot run verifiers in 4-bit on SLMs.

Insight 2: The Imbalance Illusion

Look at DeepSeek-R1 (16-bit) on GSM8K. It has a seemingly bad FPR of 64.1%, but an incredible MCC of 0.354 (compared to standard models).

Because its generator is 90% accurate, 90% of the traces it evaluates are correct. Making just a handful of mistakes artificially inflates the FPR percentage. MCC proves that DeepSeek is actually a phenomenal binary classifier.

5. Final Conclusion

Self-Improvement on Edge Devices is Architecturally Bound by Model Size.

The MCC data unequivocally proves that you cannot compress a non-reasoning verifier below ~3 Billion parameters and expect it to self-correct. These models act as "Yes Men", verifying their own broken logic with high confidence.

If you are building an autonomous, self-correcting agentic pipeline on edge hardware, Llama-3.2-3B and Qwen3.5-2B are the absolute minimum viable architectures. Attempting self-improvement loops on anything smaller, particularly with 4-bit quantization, will result in catastrophic degradation.

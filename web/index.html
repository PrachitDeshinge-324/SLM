Project dashboard

Small language models are rarely run at full precision: their weights are rounded to 8 or 4 bits to fit on cheap hardware. Self-improvement methods let a model train on answers its own samples agree on. Experiment D asks whether rounding the weights breaks the agreement signal that self-improvement depends on.

The pages run from the question and what is known, to the results, to what comes next. The two results pages are drawn from the run files in results/ and regenerated on request, so they never need editing by hand.

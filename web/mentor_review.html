Executive Summary: Multi-Round Self-Training & Verification in Quantized SLMs

Key Empirical Findings (1-Minute Read)

  • The Experimental Scope: Multi-round self-training and test-time verification across Qwen3.5-0.8B and Qwen3.5-2B under both 4-bit NF4 and 16-bit bfloat16 precision.
  • Round 1 Peak vs. Multi-Round Degradation: All models improve at Round 1 across both Pass@1 (+2% to +7%) and Majority Vote (+2% to +4%). However, performance consistently degrades in subsequent rounds (R2 and R3) as errors accumulate in the unlabelled pool.
  • The Error Mechanism (Label Drift): Without external ground truth, models train on their own hallucinations. Plurality label accuracy in the pool drops from 96% → 87%, tripling the volume of false training targets ($5 \rightarrow 17 \rightarrow 31$).
  • The "Inverted Tool-Use" Penalty (Python PoT): While frontier LLMs thrive on Program-of-Thought (PoT), sub-3B quantized models experience an inversion effect: executed Python code drops accuracy by -21.0 pts (81.0% → 60.0% on 2B 4-bit) due to high execution failure rates (43.4%).

4. External Symbolic Verification: The "Inverted Tool-Use" Penalty

A natural hypothesis to break this error loop is grounding reasoning in an external symbolic executor (Python interpreter). We evaluated Program-Aided Language modeling (PAL / PoT) across 200 GSM8K questions.

Empirical Discovery: Program-of-Thought Degrades SLMs by 20 Points

Unlike 70B+ frontier models which gain +15–20% from code generation, sub-3B quantized models experience an execution failure cliff and logic degradation:

  • Qwen3.5-2B (4-bit) Executed PoT:
    • Execution Validity: 67.7% of code traces ran cleanly. 27.7% were truncated by a 200-token cap (causing 85.7% of all crashes).
    • Accuracy on Completed Code: Even when isolating non-truncated code that ran cleanly, Pass@1 is only 56.7% and Consensus Vote is 60.5% (vs. 81.0% for natural language CoT, a -20.5 pt penalty).
  • Qwen3.5-0.8B (4-bit) Executed PoT (512 token ceiling):
    • Execution Validity: 90.7% ran cleanly (cutoff was only 1.1%).
    • Accuracy: 26.8% Pass@1 and 37.0% Majority Vote (vs. 56.0% for natural language CoT, a -19.0 pt penalty).

5. The Root Cause: Pseudo-Label Degradation in the Pool

Why do multi-round self-training loops break down without ground truth? The model becomes overconfident in its own high-frequency errors.

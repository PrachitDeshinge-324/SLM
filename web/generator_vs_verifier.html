Generator and verifier accuracy

  • Single: the generator's single answer is the share of all sampled answers that are right. The verifier's single answer is one answer picked among those the model itself approved.
  • Voted: the generator's vote is the majority answer of its samples. The verifier's vote is the majority among the approved samples only.
  • Δ is verifier minus generator, in points: green when the verifier is higher, red when it is lower. The small line under it is a 95% bootstrap interval over questions; when it includes zero, the difference may be noise.

How these numbers are made

  • Each model is scored on the same 100 questions at every precision (the first ones every precision completed, as on the Phase 2 page), with all 8 samples of each. The verifier is the same model at the same precision, judging those samples.
  • A sample cut off at the token limit counts as wrong and does not vote. If the verifier approves nothing for a question, its vote falls back to the plain vote and its single answer to a random sample.
  • Answers are re-extracted and verdicts re-parsed with the current code. Reference answers are used only to score. Regenerate with python scripts/export_generator_vs_verifier.py.

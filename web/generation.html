At a glance

Mean change from the 16-bit run of the same model, on the questions both runs contain. A cell is one model at one sample count. Quantized runs that cannot be paired, or share fewer than 50 questions with their 16-bit run, are listed below the table and not averaged in.

Definitions

Pass@k coverage

$$\text{Pass@}k = 1 - \frac{\binom{N - c}{k}}{\binom{N}{k}}$$

The probability that at least one of $k$ sampled paths is correct ($c$ correct out of $N$). The tables use $k = N$, the Pass@N column: at least one of the $N$ samples is correct. It measures whether the model has enough coverage for a self-improvement loop to find a correct path.

Agreement

$$\text{Agreement} = \frac{\text{Count}(\text{Majority Answer})}{\text{Valid samples}}$$

The share of complete samples that give the most common answer. It is the confidence signal that self-consistency and vote-filtered training rely on.

  • Voted accuracy: the majority answer is correct.
  • Single-sample accuracy: the share of all $N$ samples that finished and are correct. A truncated sample counts as wrong. Voting can hide damage that this measure shows.
  • Diversity: the mean number of distinct answers per question.
  • Truncated: the share of samples that hit the token limit. They never vote. Red above 5%.

Results by dataset

Changes are in percentage points against 16-bit with a 95% paired bootstrap interval over questions. Green or red means the interval excludes zero.

Calibration: does agreement still mean correct?

When the samples agree more, is the majority answer right more often? The closer a line is to the diagonal, the better agreement works as a confidence. Compare the precisions of one model.

    How these numbers are made

    • Every run is re-scored from its raw samples with the current answer extractor and matcher. Metrics stored inside the run files are not used.
    • A quantized run is compared with the 16-bit run of the same model, dataset and sample count, on the questions both contain, and only when both used the same token limit.
    • Runs differ in date, prompt version and, for 8-bit, number format. See section 2.2 of RESEARCH_DOSSIER.md before reading small differences as an effect of precision.

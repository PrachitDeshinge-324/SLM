Phase 1: Generator Quantization & Sample Scaling

Executive Summary

Before testing verifiers, we must understand how quantization affects the base model's raw generation capability. The main findings:

  • 8-bit is highly robust: Across models, 8-bit accuracy is nearly identical to 16-bit.
  • 4-bit drops accuracy, but increases diversity: 4-bit noise forces models to explore more varied paths.
  • Compute (N=16) mitigates 4-bit loss: By doubling the samples, 4-bit models often find the correct mathematical path (Pass@k), surpassing 16-bit N=8 baselines.
